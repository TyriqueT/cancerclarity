LESSON 01 · EVERYDAY CHOICES

Tobacco and cancer

Learn why avoiding tobacco is an important way to reduce cancer risk.

Tobacco smoke contains chemicals that can damage DNA. Smoking causes several types of cancer. Avoiding tobacco and getting support to quit can help reduce risk.

Explore tobacco information ↗
☀️

LESSON 02 · SUN SAFETY

Protect your skin

Understand how ultraviolet (UV) radiation can affect your skin.

UV radiation from the sun and tanning devices can damage skin. Shade, protective clothing, sunglasses, and broad-spectrum sunscreen can help reduce UV exposure.

Explore sun safety ↗
🛡️

LESSON 03 · VACCINATION

Know about vaccines

Discover how certain vaccines can help prevent infections linked to cancer.

HPV vaccination helps prevent infections from HPV types that cause most HPV-related cancers. Hepatitis B vaccination can help prevent hepatitis B infection, which can cause liver cancer. Ask a qualified healthcare professional about recommendations for you.

Explore CDC vaccine information ↗
🗓️

LESSON 04 · EARLY DETECTION

Understand screening

Learn what screening means and why recommendations can differ by person.

Screening looks for certain cancers before symptoms appear. Recommended tests depend on factors such as age, personal and family history, and the type of cancer. Not every cancer has a recommended screening test.
